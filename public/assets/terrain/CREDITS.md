# Terrain texture credits

All ground mats and the table top are derived from CC0 (public domain) textures from Poly Haven, downloaded as 2k JPGs
and then de-tiled, tinted and combined with procedural noise features by `art/board-textures/gen.py`. No other source
images are used.
