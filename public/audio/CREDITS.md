# Audio credits

All audio in this folder was generated for Whirr Machine with ElevenLabs, from prompts and lines written for this
project (see `tools/audio-manifest.json`):

- Sound effects: ElevenLabs Sound Effects.
- Narrator lines: ElevenLabs text to speech, voice "Harry - Fierce Warrior", model `eleven_flash_v2_5`.
- Music (`music/`): ElevenLabs Music (`music_v1`, instrumental). Two candidates per track (`-a`, `-b`) for the
  owner to choose from.
