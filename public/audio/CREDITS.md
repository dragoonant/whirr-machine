# Audio credits

All audio in this folder was generated for Whirr Machine with ElevenLabs, from prompts and lines written for this
project (see `tools/audio-manifest.json`):

- Sound effects: ElevenLabs Sound Effects.
- Narrator lines: ElevenLabs text to speech, voice "Harry - Fierce Warrior", model `eleven_flash_v2_5`.
- Music (`music/`): ElevenLabs Music (`music_v1`, instrumental). Two candidates per track (`-a`, `-b`) for the
  owner to choose from.
- Faction battle themes (`music/music-faction-<key>.mp3`, 60 s loops, `music_v1`, instrumental): Trollbloods, Circle
  Orboros, Cryx and Menoth, one each, prompts in `tools/audio-manifest.json`. Suggested trims (RMS target 0.125):
  trollbloods 0.63, circle 0.82, cryx 0.45, menoth 0.70.

- M9 factions and fury (38 clips: warbeast steps and growls, bone-jack and crusader steps, claws, bite, chain, hammer,
  holy flame, soul shot, flame jets, arc node, choir hum, thorns, fury leach/force/frenzy/transfer, trooper and beast
  deaths, narrator faction, leader and fury lines): same tools and voice, about 2.7k credits.
